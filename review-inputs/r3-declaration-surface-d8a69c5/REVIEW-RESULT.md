## 5. A–E assessment

### A. Structural convenience versus scientific meaning

REPORT.md Sections 3–6 and DEFERRED.md explicitly limit the exercise to one
dataset/design, full represented-observation admission, and one family/slot per
analysis. These are local layout restrictions. The example has one observation
per group and claims no sample variance, degrees of freedom, F statistic or
computability. Required true independence/non-flattening declarations are admission
to this exercise, never proof of reality. Unknown variance and after/unknown
selection remain representable, as independently confirmed by the combined timing
and zero-vector positive case. No method is selected from observed data.

### B. Identifiers, ownership and contamination

`check.mjs` uses separate Maps for groups, units, observations, analyses and
families, detects duplicate object IDs, and validates reciprocal family ownership.
Result IDs are unique; a result binds to analysis, family, Contract and local
member coverage. Family-local member names are not global hypothesis identities.

The reviewer cases accept consistent instance renaming, a member rename confined
to one family, complete family swaps with all reciprocal/result links updated,
and consistent Contract/result-kind swaps. They reject incomplete variants and
cross-family references even where both families use m-0/m-1/m-2. A wholly coherent
replacement document is structurally valid; the checker does not authenticate the
author's original intent or extra-document provenance. REPORT.md Sections 6–7
and the base provenance boundary do not claim that stronger protection.

The module loads `fixture-contracts.json` relative to its own file and exposes
only `check(text)`. Producer data cannot replace that fixed catalog. The injected
catalog field fails schema validation; a consistently changed but unknown Contract
label fails relations. Exact synthetic labels bind only result/family shape, not
scientific validity or real Contract registration. There is no remote resolution.

### C. Families and results

All-pairs coverage checks every distinct declared group pair, rejects self/unknown
endpoints and reversed duplicates, while retaining explicit direction. The
reviewer's fourth-group positive case prevents reliance on the three-group example
alone. Missing pairs fail even when the associated result member is also removed.
Many-to-one requires the declared control as subtrahend and every other group once;
a coherent control change passes and a reversed control edge fails.

Finite vectors require one coefficient for each declared group. Coefficient sum,
nonzero constraints, rescaling and numerical/hypothesis equivalence are intentionally
not tested. Distinct member IDs with identical vectors pass as documented, without
establishing distinct hypotheses. `planned_contrast` is a prospective slot category:
its accepting after/unknown selection does not qualify the analysis as planned.

The six disjoint result categories have deferred payloads. Unknown p-value,
confidence, tolerance and guarantee fields fail. Omnibus, pairwise, many-to-one,
planned-contrast, adjustment and interval bindings do not conflate FWER, FDR or
coverage. Adjustment-only use passes without an omnibus analysis. The present
shell still requires dataset/design; DEFERRED.md expressly leaves raw-p-value-only
attachment policy open and does not impose that shell permanently on generic
p-value correction.

### D. Input and failure behavior

`check.mjs` calls the existing strict parser on text before using its parsed value,
then JCS before schema/relation checks. Duplicate decoded keys, malformed Unicode
and negative-zero tokens fail at the raw stage; overflow to infinity fails the
numeric stage. AJV explicitly disables coercion, field removal and defaults and
uses closed objects. The reviewer checked nested escaped duplicates, malformed
Unicode keys, negative-zero exponent and negative underflow-to-zero tokens,
nonfinite array values, malformed syntax, and combined absent/incorrect fields.

All-arrays reversal preserved relation validity while changing canonical bytes.
JCS is not an array sorter, and the candidate is not canonical-byte Record ingress.
Every one of the 28 reviewer-selected pairs of independent relation defects was
confirmed JSON-Schema-valid and rejected without an uncaught exception. This is
finite evidence, not a universal proof for arbitrary sizes or programmatic objects.
The schema guards the member/result variants before relation dereferences; missing
referents in the relation loops are guarded. No exception or false success was
found within the reviewed text-input cases.

Local diagnostics remain exercise labels. Their alphabetical presentation is not
an adopted first-failure order. Existing raw-parser behavior is reused rather than
issuing new R3 reason codes. Resource admission, arbitrary-size execution guarantees
and arbitrary JavaScript-object inputs remain outside this candidate's claim.
