RFC Summary and Research Gate; cumulative C.3/C.10, Z.3, AA.2 and AC.2.

while asking H1 to establish independent source/claim mapping. It correctly flags
independence limitations in general, but does not give a complete operative
resolution for these concrete cases:

   AA.2's James/Brown review also does not establish a distinct-model Welch pass.

   withholds its missing original-source scope. No inspected fixed return maps
   the Welch characterization to a qualifying independent primary-source review.
   This is missing demonstrated review coverage, not a finding that no such review
   could exist elsewhere or that the approximate F characterization is false.
2. **SR-K/SR-G.** The opening table describes accepted interval/contrast findings
   and bounded FDR reuse. PR #192 still records model-level PENDING. The later
   continuation at `4087ca5` Section 8 correctly reconciles mixed assistance as
   compatible, but leaves the scoped determination to the acceptance record.
   The inspected SR-B/SR-C successor decisions explicitly leave this question
   separate. The fixed opening package supplies no precise successor locator
   disposing of that state. Historical status summaries reporting approval do not
   identify its exact evidence scope. No prior approval is cancelled by this review;
   the missing item is its durable, claim-bounded connection to this package.

- For OMN-02, link an existing qualifying review if available. Otherwise explicitly
  treat its inherited characterization as unconfirmed context and exclude it from

  approximation boundary, with actual investigator/model/context disclosure.

  if it exists; record its evidence and scope faithfully instead of requesting the
  approval again. Do not interpret mixed assistance as a requirement for exclusive

  explicitly exclude the affected positive claims from opening premises while
  retaining their candidate/research dispositions, or perform only the missing
  bounded source pass with documented separation.
- State which recent Ge/James/Brown/Troendle findings are comparative context or
  exclusions, rather than independent scientific support enabling a Contract.
  Their transfer/research status and conservative limits do not, by themselves,
  require another full review before opening. A distinct-model pass is required
