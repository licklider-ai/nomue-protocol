## 7. Outcome-existence and observation-state boundary

**Method record for the absence assessment (`AC-18`).** The inspected
artifacts and complete ranges are: `AE-01`, `ENV/JM/MONO(2000)7` edition, PDF
pages 1-39; `AE-02r`, March 2022 edition, PDF pages 1-44; `AE-03`, the
published article pages 1555-1577 (PDF pages 1-23). The method was: complete
page-by-page extraction of each PDF's text layer; full reading of every
definitional, guiding-principle, procedural, endpoint, monitoring, and record
section so identified; page-image inspection where the text layer was
degraded; and case-insensitive word-boundary searches over the complete
extracted texts for the Section 9 commissioned terms and, additionally, for
`missing`, `missingness`, `imputation`, `imputed`, `MCAR`, `MAR`, `MNAR`,
`censor`, `nonexistent`, `mechanism`, and `statistical analysis`. Because the
assessment rests on this extraction-plus-search-plus-targeted-reading method
rather than a verified linear read of every sentence, the absence claim is
graded `CROSS_SOURCE_INFERENCE`, not `VERIFIED_DIRECT`.

Within that scope: no inspected source defines a post-event scheduled outcome
as conceptually existing, unobserved, prevented-but-defined, or nonexistent,
and none defines a statistical missingness mechanism. The search hits for the
supplementary terms are non-statistical or peripheral: `missing` occurs once
in `AE-01` ("missing or abnormal teeth", Annex 4, p. 38); `MAR`, `MCAR`, and
`MNAR` have zero word-boundary hits in all three texts; `mechanism` hits in
`AE-03` are biological; and `censor` occurs once, in `AE-02r` p. 27, where
the guidance reports a cited submission that truncated longevity studies can
be analysed with proportional-hazards models on censored data — a
design-option mention attributed to cited literature, defining neither
existence semantics nor a missingness mechanism. Per the commission, no
hidden outcome after euthanasia is inferred.

What the corpus does define directly: the event and its time are first-class
recorded facts distinct from the observation stream (`AE-01` p. 26; `AE-02r`
pp. 10-11); an animal found dead and an animal humanely killed produce
differently provenanced final records (`AE-01` p. 26); and an absent
observation caused by a life-support systems failure is classed as an
unexpected negative outcome rather than an anticipated intervention point
(`AE-02r` pp. 4-5).
