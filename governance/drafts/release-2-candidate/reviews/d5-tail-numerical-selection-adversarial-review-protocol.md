# D5 tail numerical selection candidate adversarial-review protocol

## Review objective

Independently determine whether the exact implementation head soundly selects the
already reviewed input-specific Student-t tail truth-error proof as the Release 2
tail numerical-contract candidate while leaving platform, support, runtime, and
authority decisions open.

The review must return `GO` or `NO-GO`. `GO` applies only to this M2-B selection
increment. It does not close M2 until the exact reviewed head is merged and the
review result is durably recorded.

## Exact identity and delta

Record the exact base commit, implementation head, tree, parent list, changed paths,
and line delta. Confirm that the branch is derived from the then-current `main` and
contains no unrelated lane changes.

The intended scope is limited to:

- the tail numerical selection checkpoint;
- its fail-closed validator and focused tests;
- this review protocol; and
- the minimum numerical-readiness metadata and validator/test synchronization.

No numerical formula, operation graph, table cell, evidence byte, p-value result,
projection arithmetic, or bound arithmetic should change.

Resolve and inspect the repository artifacts rather than trusting this protocol's
summary. At minimum verify:

1. the reviewed runtime-series graph and its independent disposition;
2. the reviewed inverse-beta table and exact content hash
   `sha256:ba1f992199e9e153956589d62dcf5a6509575100bb7c923c170bfa45fdd76c08`;
3. the reviewed table-to-graph integration;
4. the reviewed input-specific truth-error proof, repair, and close-review chain;
5. the reviewed supported-execution trace binding and exact-primitive verifier; and
6. the projection-margin helper as exercised in the truth-error review.

Confirm that finite corpora, including the df 197 374-cell witness under its
2,978-cell candidate bound and the df 200 5,182-iteration case, remain observations
or pointwise proof examples and are not promoted into a global bound.

## Selection proposition to attack

The candidate proposition is conditional:

- use the reviewed input-specific bound produced from the same verified execution
  trace as the returned tail value;
- require the exact reviewed inverse-beta table binding;
- require exact primitive verification and every proof precondition;
- for series branches, require the reviewed binary64 stagnation observation before
  the iteration cap and the reviewed positive a-posteriori remainder bound;
- accept only `positive_normal` or `rounded_one` projection classes whose distance
  to the nearest policy-class transition is strictly greater than the
  input-specific integer-cell bound; and
- otherwise refuse without a support or correctly-rounded-output claim.

Determine independently whether this conditional rule needs a global constant
truth-error bound. The expected candidate conclusion is that it does not: a sound
per-execution bound compared with the same execution's projection margin closes the
tail numerical question pointwise. A global constant may be a later optimization
or coarse prefilter, but no finite corpus maximum may supply it.

## Mandatory adversarial battery

At minimum attack:

- input-specific selection demotion;
- global-bound requirement or selection promotion;
- finite-corpus maximum promotion;
- table hash, table path, or review-disposition substitution;
- trace/value/proof decoupling;
- bypass of exact primitive verification;
- iteration-cap acceptance without the reviewed termination observation;
- omission or weakening of either remainder rule;
- non-strict projection-margin comparison;
- acceptance of zero, subnormal, invalid, or insufficient-margin projections;
- M2 closure before independent review;
- supported df, platform, execution-predicate, domain, or runtime promotion;
- final reason-code freeze, Public Check/bundle issuance, R2-D5 completion, or
  Release 2 completion; and
- hidden own properties, symbol keys, accessors, sparse or extended arrays,
  throwing proxies, and cycles.

Instrument accessor attacks and require zero caller-provided getter invocations.
All hostile programmatic inputs must return a deterministic nonempty validation
error without throwing.

## Readiness consistency

Confirm that readiness records only a selection candidate pending independent
review. It may record that the input-specific form is selected for the tail
numerical contract and that a global constant is unnecessary for this closure.
It must keep all of the following unchanged or explicitly false/null/pending:

- supported degrees-of-freedom maximum;
- supported platform matrix;
- selected supported-execution predicate;
- supported domain;
- runtime support;
- runtime projection-margin activation;
- final reason codes;
- Public Check and bundle issuance;
- R2-D5 completion; and
- Release 2 completion.

The previously reviewed truth-error proof checkpoint remains a historical proof
record; this increment must not rewrite its mathematical implementation or claim
that the prior review itself selected the bound.

## Required execution

Run the focused selection and readiness tests and full `pnpm check`. Confirm the
worktree is clean after the run and that generated-file checks, conformance, Release
1 invariance, and authority guards remain green. Inspect all hosted CI jobs for the
exact implementation head.

## GO criteria

Return `GO` only if:

1. exact identity and scope match;
2. the evidence chain supports every selected condition;
3. the global-constant conclusion is justified without corpus promotion;
4. the validator fails closed under the mandatory mutation battery;
5. readiness and nonclaims are internally consistent;
6. full checks and exact-head CI are green; and
7. RFC #25 remains open with no authority or support leakage.

Any understated condition, fail-open checkpoint shape, numerical behavior change,
or premature support/authority transition is a blocker. Do not merge the semantic
selection before an independent `GO` on the exact head.
