## Retained limitations

No formal B-2 close, new human review, distinct served-model attestation or new
original-PDF reading is asserted. The numerical recommendation is reused only
for unchanged covered bytes/domain. New wrapper semantics need their own bounded
review. The proposal does not certify total host memory, non-root execution,
supervisor-crash recovery, p generation or scientific familywise error control.

Schema ownership is exact structural accounting, not proof that 17 prose clauses
fully specify every semantic relation. Nine fixed fixtures complement the wider
candidate tests, not complete all future conformance requirements. Prospective
registry rows remain non-operative and include a new versioned policy choice.
Current evidence/CI state is recorded in VALIDATION.json and the PR, not inferred
from old runs. An archive row is not a new execution result.
