## 1. Recommendation and current boundary

It does not require F, Studentized-range or multivariate-t computation. Alternatives are **PVL-01 Bonferroni** for the smallest arithmetic slice and **PVL-07 Hochberg** if the intended family's Simes/independence basis can first be covered. These are proposals for the manager to select, not selected methods.

This ordering is a dependency-based inference from the fixed evidence, not a measured effort estimate or claim of current implementation eligibility. PVL-01 has a particularly clear later scoped review/acceptance chain; if the manager's sole objective is the shortest path with that chain, it is a reasonable first choice. ## 6. P-value input boundary and numerical work left separate

Two different questions remain explicit:

1. **Arithmetic transformation:** do the recorded member IDs, represented p-values and exact procedure produce the claimed adjusted values/trace under the selected numerical contract?
2. **Scientific inference:** are the input p-values valid for the declared nulls and selection process, and do the variant's dependence/model conditions hold?

The first does not establish the second. Values in [0,1], a digest, a library name, or a successful recomputation do not establish super-uniformity, prospective family selection or scientific validity. Bind origin and claim scope; keep supplied-p transformation distinct from an end-to-end raw-data check. Do not implicitly dereference a source URI or run Record-supplied code. Rounded/approximate p-values may need conservative input bounds or a narrower arithmetic-only claim; this choice is not settled here.

The fixed numerical report Section 4.2 supplies preliminary reusable observations for rational arithmetic, ties, representation, cancellation and resource growth. They are **not** newly replayed or certified. A possible candidate can use exact dyadic input interpretation and integer/rational transforms, but no arithmetic graph or projection is adopted here. The level identity (for example a rational versus represented decimal), equality boundary, cumulative scan, caps, original-order mapping, precision/resource ceilings and comparison policy all require an explicit reviewed candidate. Do not derive a rejection guarantee solely from rounded adjusted output. Rejection/interval equivalence is optional and separately reviewable; the p-only first slice need not add confidence intervals.

All NB-01–NB-10 remain as recorded in the opening map. NB-04/05/09 affect the algebraic first slice; NB-08 applies if output/decision or interval equivalence is claimed. NB-01's withdrawn B2/C3/G bounds do not make unaffected scalar observations false, but neither do they certify NF-B. Range and multivariate-t dependent feasibility remains withdrawn. Noninteger df, missing numerical-source claims, maximum-modulus routes, Dunnett tables and transferred randomness work retain their own bounds; none becomes a universal prerequisite for unrelated p-only arithmetic. A new scientific proof, a full original reread or numerical callback repair belongs in a separately scoped commission.

## 7. Structural requirements to carry to Lane 4

These are design-review inputs under the existing RFC, not issued fields or new authority:

- Preserve exact Contract / Profile / schema / Public Check / bundle roles. R2 reuse stays conditional; do not import unissued identifiers or reinterpret historical bundles.
- Separate family identity from member identity and from order in a procedure. Bind the complete family or exact generator, m, each hypothesis, raw input and original result order. Ties need deterministic traces without changing member decisions by label order.
- Represent whether inputs are raw observations, bound summaries or externally supplied p-values and what is actually recomputed. Preserve p-value origin, sidedness, null and sampling/variance assumptions as claim-bearing information. Numeric validity does not prove scientific validity.
- Keep omnibus, all-pairs, explicit-control, fixed contrasts, contrast space/selection and intersection families distinct. k groups, m tests, rank and degrees of freedom are different quantities. A family of one test does not license a two-group R3 design.
- Separate arithmetic outcome, conditional scientific guarantee, FWER/FDR/coverage criterion, approximation status and unsupported claims. Do not emit a single overall VERIFIED/scientific-validity scalar or an unreviewed generic significance field.
- Keep step-down, step-up and closed-testing traces distinct; define threshold ownership, stop rule, cumulative direction, equality and zero/one behavior. Do not let an output cap silently change endpoint decisions.
- For closure, bind every required intersection and its local test; no Record-supplied executable test or incomplete family accepted by default. Bound family and operation sizes before expensive arithmetic.
- For intervals, bind estimates, covariance/scale, sidedness, critical family, quantile definition and the exact covered contrast universe. Interval/test duality needs one reviewed comparison surface, not unrelated adjusted p-values attached to arbitrary intervals.
- Keep exact input interpretation, mathematical target, numerical projection and comparison policy separate. Levels and tolerances belong to check versions; define deterministic refusal order and resource/execution admission. Preserve source/review/acceptance links and unresolved exclusions as evidence metadata, without minting a parallel gate registry.
