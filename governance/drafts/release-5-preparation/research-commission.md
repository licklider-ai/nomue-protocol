# Release 5 declared-design selection research commission

Status: informative Research Gate commission. This document authorizes evidence
collection and review only. It does not freeze design, issue Protocol meaning or
open public discussion.

## Research question

For the bounded family set of independent two-group, paired two-condition and
independent multi-group continuous-outcome analyses, what explicit design facts and
decision distinctions are necessary to make a finalized analysis-Contract selection
reviewable and independently checkable without inferring design from observed values
or software defaults?

## Required independent pass

Use a fresh investigator context separate from the proposal author. Statistical and
methodological claims require primary-source review. Record the model/provider or
human role when material, exact sources, pages or sections, access limits, date,
review scope and immutable repository target.

Do not treat an additional pass in the authoring context as independent research.

## Questions to answer

1. Which facts distinguish independent groups, paired observations and independent
   multi-group designs without relying on observed outcome values?
2. Which facts must be declarations because they cannot be established from the
   Record values alone?
3. Can successful selection be defined without conflating scientific assumptions,
   structural conformance and numerical computability, and is any separate portable
   negative-selection artifact necessary in the first slice?
4. What pre-data or pre-analysis timing declarations are necessary to prevent silent
   data-dependent family selection?
5. What ambiguity or overlap remains when exactly two groups are present, pairing is
   absent or contradictory, units appear more than once, or the analysis population
   differs from the supplied dataset?
6. Which selection facts can be independently checked, and which remain attributed
   but unproved researcher declarations?
7. Does a principal-attribution reference belong in the first Protocol slice, or can
   the Protocol remain limited to a finalized declaration whose authentication stays
   outside the Record?
8. What non-claims are necessary to prevent a passed selection check from being read
   as proof of assumption truth, causal validity or overall scientific correctness?
9. Which parts of the result can be reused across the three families, and which must
   remain Contract-specific?
10. What facts would require reopening this research when rank, repeated, clustered
    or other families are later added?

## Source requirements

- Prefer original methodological literature, authoritative standards and official
  guidance relevant to the exact claim.
- Separate source-established facts, investigator inference and proposed Protocol
  convention.
- Record material disagreement and failed source acquisition.
- Do not use a software package's default behavior as authority.
- Do not infer general method-selection validity from the correctness of a single
  calculation or implementation.

## Deliverable

Return an English report containing:

1. exact target commit and files reviewed;
2. source inventory with stable identities and inspected locations;
3. answers to the ten questions above;
4. a proposed minimal declaration set and counterexamples;
5. a matrix separating conformance, selection, admissibility, computability and
   numerical verification;
6. unresolved holds and reopen conditions;
7. assessment of whether the RFC candidate may proceed unchanged, should be narrowed
   or should be rejected; and
8. independence and access disclosures.

The report does not allocate Requirement IDs or design schema fields unless the
evidence clearly supports their meaning. A coherent report is not itself steward
acceptance or public-opening authority.
