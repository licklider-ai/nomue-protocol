## Primary-source completion packet

Prioritize classical calibration and any retained interval claim. The investigator
receives the question and required evidence below, not a requirement to vindicate
one preferred paper or implementation. Cochran and Yates are candidate originals;
an appropriate alternative original can support a claim if its coverage is
explicitly mapped and independently reviewed. A catalogue entry, abstract or
failed acquisition attempt is not completed source inspection.

Return, for each decision-bearing claim:

1. Bibliographic identity, source version, lawful acquisition route, retrieval or
   supplied-copy date, exact file hash and pages actually inspected. Keep supplier
   statements separate from observed facts; do not infer who operated a download.
2. The claim supported by those pages, its assumptions and any limits or conflicts.
   Distinguish a mathematical derivation, a historical attribution and a Protocol
   choice. Use page images when a formula or transcription is ambiguous.
3. An independent derivation connecting the source to four equal cells, the three
   proposed signed targets and the corresponding null hypotheses. Check the
   positive residual-variance case separately from zero residuals; an arithmetic
   infinity or NaN is not a distributional decision.
4. A separate disposition for S1, S2, S5 and P1 insofar as each was inspected.
   Report uninspected items explicitly. Do not close S3/S4/S6 by omission.
5. Identity and independence disclosures under AGENTS.md and governance/RFC.md.
   Author-side algebra is not the separate model/context primary-source review.

The already accepted NIST/LAPACK provided-copy status remains
COMPLETE_ON_PROVIDED_COPIES. Do not impose a new independent-download condition
on that accepted bounded record. New source evidence needs its own traceability;
two independent retrievals of a dynamic page need not have identical raw bytes.
Record raw HTML and extracted text separately, with their respective hashes when
available; identify any missing form. Content comparison does not erase that
traceability distinction or prove an unobserved acquisition route.

## Opening versus later implementation

Before an opening decision: obtain reviewed support for the actual inferential
claims; decide exact scope and exclusions; reconcile R3 dependencies; complete
the RFC artifact/requirement and tier assessment; and review the assembled input.
An investigator can return a justified narrower recommendation, but this map
neither adopts it nor waives the existing research commissions.

During discussion, where the preparation rules permit: numerical algorithms,
supported domains, projection, comparison tolerances, resource ceilings, execution
predicates and final implementation fixtures can remain explicit holds. No
supported Contract or check is issued while its dependent choices remain open.
A postponed implementation proof cannot substitute for missing scientific support.

## Author-side adversarial checks

inferential validity; removing an existing source/comparison obligation through

automatic permission. The dispositions above preserve those distinctions.
This inspection is not an independent review or an opening verdict.
