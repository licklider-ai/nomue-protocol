## Proposed first discussion package

Discuss one bounded fixed-factor 2 by 2 analysis with four equally replicated
cells and independent units. Retain all four full-model columns. Define the
population cell means, signed contrasts and sample estimates explicitly as in
[the normal-model source result](normal-model-source-result.md). The proposed
inferential model has independent normal errors with one common positive
variance. Neither a declaration nor a calculation proves those assumptions true.

Propose three separate marginal F upper-tail quantities for the exact full-model
contrasts, each with numerator df one and residual df 4(n-1), after independent
source review. Report the signed estimates alongside them so squaring does not
hide direction. Each null is its own population contrast being zero; the other
main effect or interaction is not automatically removed or set to zero.

For this first opening proposal, exclude confidence intervals, significance
booleans, familywise/FDR guarantees, randomization/potential-outcome assertions,
historical priority claims, unequal cell counts, missing cells, random/mixed
factors, correlated units and robust/permutation alternatives. This is a proposed
bounded scope, not a finding that excluded procedures are invalid. Broader
commissions remain recorded; none is marked executed or completed by exclusion.

The continuous model describes idealized real observations. The proof does not
establish the exact same null distribution after quantization to binary64 or
selection by a future numerical admission predicate. The opening review needs to
check that the proposed guarantee describes conditional mathematical
recomputation under a declared model and does not claim validated statistical
coverage for encoded, selected or rounded real-world data. Runtime support,
representation-error treatment and the precise Public Check non-claims remain
explicit design work; calling a numerical proof pending cannot hide a changed
scientific claim.
