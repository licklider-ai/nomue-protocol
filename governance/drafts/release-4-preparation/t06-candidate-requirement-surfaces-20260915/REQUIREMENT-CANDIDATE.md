## Exact numerical contract: VERIFY-0030

This check tests declared-result consistency for balanced replicated fixed-factor
2-by-2 inference, conditional on the declared model, represented-input exact targets
and versioned supported domain. It does not test the real-world truth of the model.

Public candidate membership is `J-cost(B,S-C)`, with `B = 52969003320369754284032`
and Z-B eligibility. Cost SHA-256 is
`f1eb1fd866d3a48589c3e7d37b3536f700e4d5271f9a85834fa3575f034512d2`.
The complete formula and ledger are incorporated from [COST](https://github.com/licklider-ai/nomue-protocol/blob/66fa2bc201c86c62f21bb94825479427c24d8522/governance/drafts/release-4-preparation/t04-g4-budget-evidence-20260914/COST-DEFINITION.json) and [G5](https://github.com/licklider-ai/nomue-protocol/blob/66fa2bc201c86c62f21bb94825479427c24d8522/governance/drafts/release-4-preparation/t04-g5-full-procedure-evidence-20260914/REPORT.md).
C<=B is work admission; final membership also requires conformance, admissibility,
computability and representation gates. No standalone public n work ceiling or
host profile is introduced into membership.

Inherited G5 sections 2-6 fix the ordered procedure:

1. Strict raw input and exact routing, structural then semantic conformance.
2. Declared-model/design admissibility; equal observed n and distinct units.
3. Count preflight Cpre; Cpre>B refuses before moment arithmetic.
4. One exact represented-input graph; exact zero SSE is computability fail.
5. Full C using actual reduced exact F widths; C>B is supported-domain refusal.
6. Project fourteen arithmetic targets, checking exact magnitude against maximum
   finite and positive SSE projection against zero.
7. Three S-C tails from exact F/df; Z-B positive-tail rounding-to-zero refusal.
   Eligible membership is true only after all gates succeed.
8. All five integer, fourteen arithmetic-real and three tail comparisons, in order;
   continue after mismatch, then aggregate.
9. Bounded evidence and successful invocation/delivery prerequisite; invocation
   failure suppresses provisional completed results.

Let U=2^1074, z=U*y for finite binary64 outcomes, S_c=sum(z), Q_c=sum(z^2),
E=n*sum(Q_c)-sum(S_c^2). Cells follow factor and level order.
C_A=-S00-S01+S10+S11; C_B=-S00+S01-S10+S11; C_AB=S00-S01-S10+S11.
Means are S_c/(nU); effects C_A/(2nU), C_B/(2nU), C_AB/(nU).
Each SS=C^2/(4nU^2), SSE=E/(nU^2), residual df=4(n-1), F=(n-1)C^2/E after E>0.
Rounded displayed values never reenter this graph.

Integers use exact equality. Reals use correctly rounded binary64 nearest,
ties-to-even, then strict value equality. Decimal spellings parsing to the same
eligible value compare equally. Producers need the ability to obtain conforming
outputs; no internal algorithm is mandated. Ordinary floating-point calculations
can mismatch: this means contract disagreement, not scientific falsity. Comparison
is check-version-owned under CANON-0006 and the Charter minimum-standardization boundary.

S-C evaluates the exact upper F(1,4(n-1)) tail from exact F. G5 section 6 and RG1/RG2
pin its polynomial, exact integer signs and parity-aware rounding. At most 66 boundary
sign calls determine the code. F=0 gives p=1; repeats and zero F are still charged.
The old 128/256/512 schedule is not public semantics. A stronger private method
cannot silently change this version's prescribed observable decision capability.

Exact zero differs from rounded zero. Effects/SS/F may round nonzero exact targets
to zero; negative targets introduce no public -0 token. Received -0 is rejected,
not normalized. Declared SSE=0 is conformance fail; exact SSE=0 is computability fail;
positive SSE projecting to zero is representation/computability refusal, not mismatch.
Exact magnitude beyond maximum finite is project-convention refusal, not IEEE mandate.
Positive p projecting to zero is underflow refusal, not exact p=0 or public match.
Z-B uses exact zero-boundary comparison, including the tie. Positive subnormal p
follows R4 projection rules, without R2 normal-only policy.

## Outcomes and binding: VERIFY-0031 / CORE-0022

See [surface inventory](SURFACE-INVENTORY.md). Normally completed eligible scopes
aggregate proved fail before unresolved, then pass only if all 22 pass. Same Record
and check/version/bundle preserve observable decisions on normal completion. Actual
S-C resolves eligible targets; generic uncertainty controls are not real S-C ambiguity.
Execution failure, refusal, not_run and completed outcomes remain distinct. No global
VERIFIED judgment is created. Only admissibility completed pass/fail determines
eligible/ineligible; absent/error/not_run maps to not_evaluated. Scientific validity
remains not_asserted. Evidence binds revision, bundle, check/version, result and quantity.

## Non-guarantees

No unbalanced design, missing cells, repeated/clustered observations, random factors,
multiplicity correction, confidence intervals, causal interpretation or arbitrary
model fitting. No proof of declaration truth, independence, normality or common
variance; no joint, post-quantization or admission-conditional calibration. No promise
of completion on every hardware/runtime, whole-domain reference support or production
availability. This retains the existing scoped exclusions.
