## Benchmark conclusion proposed for independent review

No additional numerical implementation or repeat T13/T14 review is established
as necessary to match R2's actual candidate-decision benchmark. The remaining
mandatory increment is the limited A1 packet review, its concrete repairs and
review-result intake. An absence of a complete public adoption implementation is
not by itself a benchmark blocker.

After that review, the steward can determine R2-like readiness from the actual
packet and any recorded residuals. This author does not grant GO, close that
review, or change the management percentage. A2-A5 may improve adoption execution
quality but require separate scope/authorization and are not prerequisites by name.
