# RG3: procedure alternatives and author decision packet

Status: all recommendations are pending independent Research Gate. This
comparison does not select public parameters, change T03, modify the opening
RFC or implement a supported check. Source identities are in
[INPUTS.json](INPUTS.json); full costs are in [RG1](RG1-BOUNDEDNESS.md).

## 1. S-A: prescribed finite stages

A fixed positive-sum enclosure and stages give a sound contiguous range of
binary64 candidate encodings. For eligible quantities, declared code outside
the range is mismatch, an equal singleton is pass, and multiple candidates
including the declared code at the final stage are indeterminate. Being
unresolved does not prevent a mismatch if the declared code is outside ALL
remaining candidates. Execute other mandatory eligible comparisons after
such ambiguity.

Soundness depends on the reviewed integral identity, exact interval endpoint
operations and correct candidate-cell construction. Completeness of projection
is not established: no general separation from every binary64 midpoint follows
from the relative width factor `(1+2^-b)^(2a-1)`. Rational-root witnesses remain
unresolved at b512, including ordinary and normal/subnormal boundaries. They
are mathematical exact-F witnesses, not proof of raw-domain frequency.

Z-A leaves an additional eligibility refusal region. S-A with Z-B settles that
gate exactly but can still retain ordinary comparison ambiguity; its budget
then includes the exact construction even though the selected comparison stops
at an interval. Evidence needs the interval/range and honest final state.
Changing stage precision can change decisions and membership; it is a public
check-version choice if the schedule owns those decisions.

## 2. S-B: finite stages with exact terminal

Use the same staged work, then invoke exact-sign on remaining tails and on
unresolved zero eligibility. Early singleton/mismatch decisions are sound;
the terminal returns the same exact cell that a full exact procedure would.
Admit inputs against the full worst-case cost, not a promise that early exit
usually succeeds. Charge n/df, width, all stages, reductions and terminal
construction as RG1 specifies.

Within the mathematical admitted domain, normal completion resolves every tail
projection; positive zero-rounding remains refusal. Numerical tail
indeterminate no longer occurs. This is stronger than S-A's finite final
range and cannot silently replace an already fixed S-A version. Evidence can
record exact singleton or sound early endpoint evidence with terminal proof
when used. Reproducible public decisions do not require identical internal
allocations, but any eventual public evidence contract still needs fulfillment.

S-B can avoid expensive terminal construction on easy cases, yet has the
largest conservative worst-case envelope. The packet supplies no benchmark
proof that staging improves an implementation's resource profile.

## 3. S-C: exact-sign projection as the decision procedure

Construct the squared-CDF rational pair once per exact F. Resolve positive
projection eligibility and the final encoding by exact boundary tests. The
integer pair is bounded evidence, and the comparison candidate set is a
singleton on eligible normal completion. The method is deterministic and
complete for this finite integer-df mathematical domain, conditional on the
proof and prescribed admission/termination model; this is not a proof that a
reference host successfully executes every admitted Record.

No square-root precision schedule or gcd of the large squared-CDF pair is
required. Polynomial degree and coefficient/operand growth depend on n, df
and exact-F width; each binary64 boundary has bounded rational size. The
canonical construction and at-most-66 comparisons give the RG1 finite cost.
The cost envelope is lower than the staged envelope in the supplied symbolic
grid, but loose different envelopes do NOT prove an actual speed ordering.

Integer cancellation is exact, so loss of floating-point significance is not
a soundness problem; large numerator/denominator growth remains a cost risk.
Independent examination of squaring sign conditions, intermediate bounds and
worst-case sizes is still required. Larger df can make the polynomial costly
even if F has width one. The author does not infer practical support from
mathematical decidability.

## 4. Precision recommendation

AUTHOR RECOMMENDATION — PENDING INDEPENDENT RESEARCH GATE:
REMOVE AS PUBLIC SEMANTIC the 128 -> 256 -> 512 schedule if S-C is adopted.
The exact-cell decision has a finite integer construction and does not depend
on a chosen root precision. Enclosures could remain equivalent optional
internal fast paths, provided they cannot alter admission or the exact decision.

This is conditional on the S-C choice. If S-A instead becomes the public
procedure, the schedule remains semantic and needs its own justification.
The evidence does not justify KEEP solely because 128/256/512 exists. Adding
1024 resolves the selected witnesses but proves no universal midpoint
separation and is not recommended as a final answer on that basis.

## 5. D07 and aggregation remain architecture

For S-A with proven eligibility, finite-stage multiple candidate sets can
leave indeterminate. For S-B/S-C, normally completed admitted tails have exact
cells, so there is no numerical tail ambiguity; the 14 rational projections
also have exact finite decisions under their representation gates. This
concrete ideal procedure would thus have no remaining numerical indeterminate
on admitted, normally completed inputs. That does NOT delete the architectural
outcome, change its meaning or prove all future checks complete.

Other quantities/procedures in future versions may use sound incomplete
candidate sets and retain D07. Budget rejection, underflow, a missing
prerequisite, execution failure or invariant violation is not relabeled
indeterminate. Scope aggregation remains: proved fail takes priority over
comparison indeterminate after all mandatory executable comparisons; otherwise
all pass is pass, otherwise unresolved eligible comparison is indeterminate.
Dependent skipped work cannot manufacture a vacuous pass. Invocation failure
suppresses provisional completed output. No overall Record VERIFIED outcome
is introduced. The saved 22-slot controls illustrate these requirements;
implementing/reviewing a whole public report remains outside G1-G3.

## 6. Five author recommendations

Each item below is AUTHOR RECOMMENDATION — PENDING INDEPENDENT RESEARCH GATE.
None is a steward adoption or Research Gate clearance.

1. Domain candidate: J-cost(B,S-C), with RFC/T03 gates and exact positive-tail
   eligibility. Prefer an explicit count/width tradeoff tied to the whole
   procedure over inheriting 65/70 from old experiment constants. B remains
   an explicit unselected version parameter, not an omitted implementation
   default. Independent bound review and subsequent usefulness work can
   change the formula or the eventual B; this packet does not perform G4.
2. Cost predicate candidate: the RG1 full charged-integer expression,
   including early Cpre and bounded evidence. Prefer the quadratic
   multiplication/division plus Euclid charge model for its constructive
   meaning. The present conservative constants are candidates for proof
   review and tightening; no CPU-time guarantee is claimed.
3. Eligibility candidate: exact `p>2^-1075` tested with Z-B, with ties refused
   as positive underflow. Equivalent sufficient early exits may optimize it.
4. Terminal procedure candidate: S-C, because one bounded exact construction
   resolves both eligibility and rounding cells without a precision-dependent
   public decision. S-B remains a performance hypothesis; S-A remains a
   legitimate different semantics requiring its own versioned rules.
5. Precision schedule candidate: REMOVE AS PUBLIC SEMANTIC conditional on
   S-C; retain historical schedules only as experiments or equivalent internal
   paths. Do not retroactively change the historical candidate's results.

## 7. Dependencies and promotion

Domain and cost depend on the terminal procedure; terminal cost depends on
count, df and width; eligibility couples D03 to candidate-set construction;
precision owns outcomes only if finite staged uncertainty owns the decision.
T03 D01 strict projection, D02 arithmetic zero/SSE distinction, D03 positive
underflow, D04 conservative finite range and D07 remain fixed. D05/D06 do not
introduce a producer interval carrier.

A later selected procedure/domain can change public admission and final
outcomes, so its public-check version, RFC numerical hold, identifiers and
required coupled surfaces need explicit disposition after Research Gate.
This packet does not issue Requirements, change registries or claim a mere
editorial clarification. Public discussion tier/period impact belongs to that
later change assessment; no unadopted automatic clock-reset rule is used.
