work before any Profile or check uses timing declarations to gate, rank, flag,
score, or filter an analysis, or before any claim relies on those excluded

still conditions; this note does not announce blanket research closure.

under reconsideration. Deferral from the first slice is a recommendation, not
an adopted change. Such declarations can attribute what was stated but cannot
verify when a choice was actually made. A change to the four-part opening
claim requires explicit scope, compatibility, and notice-window reassessment.

and identity. An outside-domain result can be completed/fail; an additional
clearly labelled domain annotation must not replace that fail or be read as
scientific invalidity. A dependent not_run result has no fabricated outcome.
Held configuration is distinct from an executed check. The common-view repair
is proposed and still needs adoption and review; no new wire enum is issued.
