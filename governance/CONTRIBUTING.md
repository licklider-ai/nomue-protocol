# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in the nomue Protocol.

## What is currently accepted

- **Discussion**: questions, critique, use cases, falsification attempts, and
  proposals are welcome at any time. Release 1 exists to be tested against
  reality.
- **Non-normative contributions**: typo fixes, broken links, informative
  clarifications, tooling fixes, documentation infrastructure, and other changes
  that do not alter Protocol normative meaning, requirements, interpretation, or
  conformance behavior may be accepted under the applicable repository license.

## External normative contributions

External **normative contributions are not accepted without a separate written
Contributor Agreement already in effect for that contribution**.

Normative status is determined by semantic effect, not file format or outbound
license. A change to prose, code, a schema, registry, conformance artifact,
fixture, vector, or other machine-readable material is normative when its
incorporation would alter Protocol normative meaning, requirements,
interpretation, or conformance behavior.

Discussion, issue reports, feature requests, suggestions, and other feedback do
not by themselves create a Protocol patent commitment or an accepted normative
contribution. Stewards may independently implement ideas raised in discussion,
with appropriate credit, but externally authored normative material is not
incorporated without the required Contributor Agreement.

See the operative legal terms in [../LICENSE.md](../LICENSE.md), especially
Section 7, and the adopted licensing decision record in
[LICENSING-PLAN.md](LICENSING-PLAN.md).

## Mechanics

- English for all public artifacts, including commit messages.
- `pnpm check` green before any submission.
- Follow [../AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) and the directory-local `AGENTS.md` files.
